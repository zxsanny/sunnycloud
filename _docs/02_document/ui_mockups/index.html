sunnycloud

Sun over a cloud: one design for web, desktop and phone

A slightly gray light theme and a graphite dark theme. Sky blue marks everything you can act on; sun yellow marks the brand, where you are, and good news. Status colours are green, orange and red, so yellow never means "careful".
